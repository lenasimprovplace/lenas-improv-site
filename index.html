Workshops for Businesses

Improv theatre uses structured games, not performance or punchlines, to practise real skills like listening, deciding and adapting under pressure, without the usual fear of failing in front of colleagues.

I deliver on-site workshops built around four core areas: communication and active listening, leadership and adaptability, creativity and innovation, and team cohesion and trust. Sessions can focus on one area or weave several together, depending on what your team most needs.

Formats range from a high-energy two-hour taster to an extended half-day covering all four areas, for groups of 7 to 15. Well suited to offsites, onboarding or ongoing professional development.

Pricing depends on group size and session length.

What is Improv?

Improv is theatre made up on the spot, with no script, no lines to memorise and no plan to stick to. In class, that means playing games, running exercises and building scenes together in real time, using whatever your partner gives you and running with it.

You don't need to be an actor to do it. It's a hobby, plain and simple, one that happens to build real confidence, bring people together and turn strangers into friends. This isn't an acting class, it's perfect for anyone curious, nervous or just looking for a fun way to spend an evening.

Who Am I

Hi, I'm Lena.

Greek and Belgian, endlessly curious and often smiling before I even walk into the room. I lead with empathy, both on stage and in the classroom, because I think that's where the best stories and the best students come from. I've facilitated children's overnight theatre camps, trained in meditation and I'm not afraid to get a little silly to make a point.

My classroom is built on two things: safety and humour. Safety, because real growth happens when people feel free to take risks without fear of getting it "wrong." Humour, because life's too short not to laugh at yourself a little. I've taught both kids and adults, and I've learned that the same spirit of play works its magic at any age.

Cancellation Policy

  • 01 Cancel at least 72 hours before class for a full refund.
  • 02 Cancel between 48 and 72 hours before class for credit toward a future session (no refund). Credit must be used within the same month.
  • 03 Cancellations inside 48 hours aren't eligible for refund or credit.
  • 04 Advanced students who miss a class can attend a beginner class instead, at no extra cost, subject to space.

  • 06 Private at-home sessions follow the same windows.
  • 07 Sound & Story is a one-off workshop and follows the same windows: 72+ hours for a full refund, 48 to 72 hours for credit. Inside 48 hours, it's not eligible for refund or credit.
  • 08 If I need to cancel a class (illness, low enrolment, etc.), you'll first be offered a makeup class. If that doesn't work for you, you'll receive full credit or a refund, your choice.
  • 09 No-shows aren't eligible for refund or credit, but reach out, life happens and exceptions are considered case by case.

By enrolling and paying for a class or workshop, you agree to this cancellation policy.

FAQ

Do I need any experience?

No. Foundations classes (kids, teens and adults) are designed for complete beginners. Sound & Story and Advanced classes are the exceptions, they're intended for students who've completed Foundations or have equivalent improv experience.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move freely in. No costumes or special gear needed.

I'm shy, is this still a good fit?

Yes, in fact it's often exactly the right fit. Classes are built around a safe, judgement-free space. Plenty of shy students come out of their shell more through play than through pressure to perform.

Is there a minimum age?

Kids classes start at age 8. For younger children, private Play & Grow sessions at home may be a better starting point, reach out to discuss.

Can someone come watch the class?

Classes are students-only. This helps everyone focus, take risks and be fully present without an audience watching.

How do I enrol or pay?

Use the contact form or email directly and payment is handled by bank transfer or Revolut.

What's your cancellation policy?

See the full Cancellation Policy section above, but in short: 72+ hours notice gets a full refund, 48 to 72 hours gets credit and inside 48 hours isn't refundable.

Can I try a class before committing?

Yes. I'm hosting one-hour taster classes on September 21 and 28 at Sarri, Kids & Teens at 16:30 to 17:30, Adults at 18:30 to 19:30, €11 per class. A relaxed, no-pressure way to see if it's for you before signing up.

I don't know anyone else coming, is that weird?

Not at all. Most people show up solo, and lots of them make friends really quickly in class. Improv has a way of turning strangers into a group fast.

Can I request a custom or themed workshop?

Yes. Whether it's a birthday, a special event, or a themed session, get in touch and we can build something around what you're looking for.

Where can I find exact class dates and times?

Check the Schedule section above, it has a live calendar showing exactly what's on, when and where.
